03 — About me

How I work

✦ Move over me
Alpesh KherAhmedabad, IN · --:--
10+

years in UI/UX and product design, from agency delivery to in-house product. Give me a tangle of business rules, edge cases and stakeholders, and I'll find the simplest flow through it, whether that's enterprise SaaS, a design system or an AI feature.

01Complex flows, made simple+

I map the rules first, then the screens: discovery, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes and final UI, and I stay in the loop through design QA until it ships.

02Systems built to scale+

Figma libraries on shadcn foundations: variables, light and dark modes, and components with every interaction and error state accounted for, so developers never have to guess.

03Talking to real users+

Interviews, usability sessions and UX audits that I plan and run myself, with findings written up as clear scope calls the whole team can act on.

04AI inside everyday work+

Review cycles, suggested records and approval states that put AI where people already work. I also use Claude, ChatGPT and Figma AI to prototype and automate my own process.

05Brand sites that sell+

Bold, responsive, multi-region brand and e-commerce sites, designed hand in hand with React and Next.js developers so the build matches the design.

06Growing design teams+

I've led design teams of up to seven people: onboarding juniors, running reviews, and setting the shared UI guidelines that keep quality steady from project to project.
